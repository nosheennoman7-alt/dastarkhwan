Our story

A family recipe, served to Manchester

Ali's Biryani House started with one pot and a family recipe passed down through generations. Today we still cook every biryani the same way: rice and meat layered by hand, sealed and left to steam on a low flame until every grain carries the flavour.

Our meat is halal, our spices are ground in-house, and nothing leaves the kitchen until it's just right. Whether you're eating in or taking away, we want every plate to taste like home.

  • 100% HalalAll our meat is halal certified
  • Fresh dailyCooked in small batches, never reheated
  • House spicesWhole spices roasted and ground on site
